### 1. Research Purpose & Background

SafeMap AI conducts academic research on flood mitigation, real-time emergency shelter accessibility, and road network inundation in the Hyderabad Metropolitan Area. 

A critical finding from our historical flood evidence audits (spanning 1908 to 2023) is that riverine flooding along the Musi corridor (including submergence of the Moosarambagh Causeway and Chaderghat Bridge) is heavily governed by controlled reservoir releases from twin upstream impoundments: **Osman Sagar (Gandipet)** on the Musi River and **Himayat Sagar** on the Esi tributary. 

Currently, our public historical catalogue relies on post-hoc dam release press notices (e.g. "8,000 cusecs released via 4 gates") or retrospective engineering estimates. Without calibrated hourly gate opening records, dam reservoir stages, and in-stream hydrometric stage levels, coupled hydrodynamic modeling and emergency routing along the river corridor cannot be scientifically validated.
